01 / Ledger

Every movement has two sides, and they always balance.

Transfers, card spend, EMIs and splits post a debit and a credit leg. Balances are computed from the ledger, never stored as a guess.

append-onlyrunning balanceverify-ledger job
02 / Care AI

Jev decides. The language model only writes.

Each message is triaged into category, urgency and a human-handoff probability by TypeSafe Jev. The LLM drafts the reply but never makes the routing call.

decision layeragent → manager → owner
03 / Pipeline

Every transaction becomes analytics by morning.

Ledger events stream incrementally into Bronze, are cleaned and quality-checked in Silver, and land as aggregates in Gold. Airflow schedules it and PySpark runs it on Delta Lake.
